How I work

A multidimensional approach

01
Systemic therapy
Exploring how your relationships, resources and values shape your emotional health. By understanding these patterns, you can choose if you want to shift them.
02
Psychoanalytical Therapy
Working with early emotional experiences and symbols present in your narrative. Taking the unconscious into consideration.
03
Mindfulness · EFT · Schema
Practice-based, client-centered tools for emotional regulation, grounding in the present moment, and identifying and changing deep-rooted patterns of thought and behaviour.
About me

Brazilian psychologist,
In Berlin since 2010

I graduated from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ) in 2007, where I also started my journey in Psychoanalysis. I worked with teenagers and families at the European School in Berlin as a Psychosocial Counsellor for over 10 years and currently specialise in Systemic Therapy at the Systemisches Institut für Achtsamkeit (SIA).

My practice brings together psychoanalytic depth, relational focus and mindfulness - with a committed decolonial approach. My insertions in arts, music, and performance have also given me a distinctive sensitivity.
